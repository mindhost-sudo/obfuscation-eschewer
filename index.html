Field guide to eristic tactics

Aristotle's Sophistical Refutations catalogued arguments that only appear to refute. Schopenhauer's The Art of Being Right listed 38 stratagems for winning a dispute regardless of truth. This guide translates both into the patterns that show up in workplace email and chat, plus a few modern ones with the same structure.

Each tactic notes its likely root cause and a response move. The moves aim to bring the argument back to evidence; none of them are counter-tactics. A tactic in a message is a hypothesis about the message, never a verdict on the person.
